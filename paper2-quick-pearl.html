Renal 5 conditions

Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Oliguria; rising creatinine; prerenal vs intrinsic vs postrenal

Why it happens

Prerenal: low perfusion; intrinsic: tubular/glomerular injury; postrenal: obstruction.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Check potassium and ECG. Hyperkalaemia with ECG changes demands immediate escalation; consider urgent renal support.

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Abnormal kidney function or structure ≥3 months; anaemia; bone disease

Why it happens

Reduced EPO → anaemia; reduced calcitriol and phosphate retention → secondary hyperparathyroidism.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Calculate eGFR and urine ACR; beware nephrotoxins and adjust drugs to kidney function.

Nephritic Syndrome⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Cola-coloured urine; hypertension; oliguria; RBC casts

Why it happens

Inflamed glomeruli leak red cells while reduced filtration causes salt/water retention.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Think post-infectious GN, lupus, IgA nephropathy; check complement and renal function.

Nephrotic Syndrome⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Massive proteinuria; oedema; hypoalbuminaemia; hyperlipidaemia

Why it happens

Podocyte/filtration-barrier damage → protein loss, decreased plasma oncotic pressure.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Protein loss includes antithrombin: thrombosis risk. Screen urine protein and renal function.

Renal Calculi (Urolithiasis)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Colicky loin-to-groin pain; haematuria; restlessness

Why it happens

Stone causes intermittent ureteric obstruction and smooth muscle spasm.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Fever + obstructed urinary tract = infected obstruction/emergency. Noncontrast CT is highly useful.

GIT 18 conditions

Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Bilateral pitting oedema or severe wasting in child

Why it happens

Kwashiorkor associated with nutritional oedema; marasmus with severe energy deficiency.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Watch for hypoglycaemia, hypothermia, infection and dangerous refeeding electrolyte shifts.

Acute Gastroenteritis⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Acute vomiting and diarrhoea; dehydration

Why it happens

Intestinal infection alters absorption and secretion; fluid and electrolyte losses dominate risk.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Prioritise dehydration severity, oral rehydration when safe, IV fluids for shock.

Diarrhoea — Clinical Approach⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Watery vs bloody vs fatty diarrhoea; acute vs chronic

Why it happens

Secretory diarrhoea persists with fasting; osmotic diarrhoea often improves with fasting.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Blood/fever/severe pain = inflammatory cause; persistent weight loss is an alarm sign.

Food Poisoning⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Rapid vomiting after meal; food exposure cluster

Why it happens

Preformed toxins cause short-incubation vomiting; invasive organisms cause inflammation.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Staphylococcus aureus and Bacillus cereus are classic preformed-toxin clues; hydrate first.

Shigellosis (Bacillary Dysentery)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Fever; tenesmus; frequent small-volume bloody stools

Why it happens

Shigella invades colonic mucosa causing inflammatory dysentery.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Recognise dehydration and HUS-associated complications; stool testing and infection control.

Giardiasis⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Foul-smelling greasy diarrhoea; bloating; camping/untreated water

Why it happens

Giardia attaches to proximal small-bowel mucosa → malabsorption.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Stool antigen or PCR testing; consider metronidazole/tinidazole under guidelines.

Constipation⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Infrequent hard stool; straining; opioid/iron use

Why it happens

Slow transit or pelvic-floor dysfunction; medications and endocrine illness may contribute.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Obstruction signs, bleeding and weight loss demand further assessment.

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Recurrent abdominal pain associated with defecation or stool change

Why it happens

Disorder of gut–brain interaction, without structural inflammation on routine tests.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Nocturnal diarrhoea, GI bleeding, anaemia and weight loss are alarm features, not typical IBS.

Gastro-Oesophageal Reflux Disease (GORD)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Heartburn and acid regurgitation; worse lying down

Why it happens

Transient lower oesophageal sphincter relaxation permits acid reflux.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Dysphagia, bleeding, weight loss and persistent vomiting are alarm features; PPIs inhibit H+/K+-ATPase.

Peptic Ulcer Disease (PUD)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Epigastric pain; H. pylori; NSAIDs

Why it happens

Acid–mucosal defence imbalance; NSAIDs decrease protective prostaglandins.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Sudden rigid abdomen suggests perforation; test and treat H. pylori appropriately.

Upper Gastrointestinal Bleed (UGIB)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Haematemesis; melaena; tachycardia; hypotension

Why it happens

PUD, varices and erosions cause proximal gastrointestinal blood loss.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Assess ABCs, haemodynamic stability and urgent endoscopic need; blood pressure may initially be preserved.

Ulcerative Colitis (UC)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Bloody diarrhoea; urgency; continuous disease from rectum

Why it happens

Mucosal colonic inflammation; crypt abscesses; no skip lesions.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Toxic megacolon and colorectal cancer are complications; distinguish from transmural Crohn disease.

Colorectal Cancer (CRC)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Change in bowel habit; iron deficiency; rectal bleeding; weight loss

Why it happens

Adenoma–carcinoma progression or mismatch-repair defects.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Right-sided disease often causes occult bleeding/anaemia; left-sided may obstruct.

Gallstones and Biliary Colic⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Postprandial episodic right-upper-quadrant pain, no sustained fever

Why it happens

Transient cystic-duct obstruction produces biliary colic.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Persistent fever/jaundice suggests complication; ultrasonography first-line for gallstones.

Acute Cholecystitis⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

RUQ pain >6h; fever; positive Murphy sign

Why it happens

Persistent cystic-duct obstruction causes gallbladder inflammation.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Look for wall thickening/pericholecystic fluid; urgent assessment for sepsis or perforation.

Acute Pancreatitis⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Severe epigastric pain radiating to back; vomiting

Why it happens

Premature pancreatic enzyme activation drives autodigestion and inflammation.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Gallstones/alcohol are major causes; lipase ≥3× ULN supports diagnosis; watch organ failure.

Liver Cirrhosis⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Ascites; spider naevi; varices; jaundice

Why it happens

Fibrosis and regenerative nodules increase portal resistance and impair hepatic synthesis.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Low albumin and prolonged INR signal synthetic dysfunction; variceal bleed and encephalopathy are emergencies.

Ascites⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Shifting dullness; fluid wave; abdominal distension

Why it happens

Portal hypertension and sodium retention commonly cause cirrhotic ascites.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Diagnostic paracentesis for new/worsening ascites; neutrophils ≥250/µL suggests SBP.

MSK 10 conditions

Closed Long-Bone Fractures⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Pain, swelling, deformity, inability to bear weight

Why it happens

Cortical discontinuity after trauma; healing via haematoma, callus and remodelling.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Check distal neurovascular status before/after immobilisation. Pain out of proportion suggests compartment syndrome.

Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) Injury⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Twisting injury; pop; rapid haemarthrosis; instability

Why it happens

Anterior tibial translation and rotational control are impaired.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Lachman test supports diagnosis; assess associated meniscal injury.

Spinal Cord Injury⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Trauma; weakness/sensory loss; bladder dysfunction

Why it happens

Cord damage interrupts motor, sensory and autonomic pathways.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Immobilise appropriately and urgently escalate; distinguish neurogenic from spinal shock.

Osteoporosis⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Fragility fracture after low-energy trauma

Why it happens

Reduced bone strength and microarchitectural deterioration, often after menopause.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

DEXA T-score ≤−2.5; distinguish osteoporosis from osteomalacia.

Vitamin D Deficiency (Rickets/Osteomalacia)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Bone pain; proximal weakness; bowed legs in children

Why it happens

Defective mineralisation due to low calcium/phosphate availability.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Low vitamin D, elevated ALP and secondary hyperparathyroidism are classic.

Osteoarthritis (OA)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Mechanical joint pain; bony enlargement; short morning stiffness

Why it happens

Degeneration of articular cartilage with osteophytes and subchondral sclerosis.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

DIP/PIP and first CMC common; not usually marked systemic inflammation.

Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Symmetric MCP/PIP inflammatory arthritis; prolonged morning stiffness

Why it happens

Autoimmune synovitis → pannus → erosions and deformity.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

RF and anti-CCP support diagnosis; screen infection before immunosuppressive therapy.

Gout⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Sudden exquisitely painful first MTP joint; podagra

Why it happens

Monosodium urate crystal deposition activates inflammasome/neutrophils.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Needle-shaped negatively birefringent crystals; do not assume no sepsis just because crystals present.

Septic Arthritis⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Hot swollen painful joint; fever or inability to move

Why it happens

Bacterial invasion triggers destructive synovial inflammation.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Urgent aspiration, cultures and supervised antibiotics; can rapidly destroy cartilage.

Bone Tumour — Osteosarcoma⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Adolescent knee pain/swelling; metaphyseal lesion; sunburst

Why it happens

Malignant osteoid production by tumour cells.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Codman triangle and pulmonary metastasis are high-yield clues; urgent referral, no casual biopsy.

Cancer & Haematology 9 conditions

Breast Cancer⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Persistent hard irregular lump; skin tethering; nipple changes

Why it happens

Malignant breast epithelial proliferation, often ductal origin.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Triple assessment: examination, imaging, tissue sampling; recognise axillary nodes.

Cervical Cancer⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Postcoital bleeding; persistent abnormal discharge; HPV

Why it happens

High-risk HPV E6/E7 inactivate p53/Rb tumour suppressors.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

HPV vaccination and screening prevent disease; suspicious lesions need colposcopy/biopsy.

Prostate Cancer⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Older adult; LUTS or asymptomatic; abnormal DRE

Why it happens

Prostatic adenocarcinoma often arises in peripheral zone.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

PSA is not cancer-specific; bone metastases often osteoblastic.

Iron-Deficiency Anaemia⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Microcytic hypochromic cells; low ferritin; raised TIBC

Why it happens

Insufficient iron limits haem synthesis.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Look for chronic GI blood loss in adults; ferritin can rise with inflammation.

Thalassaemia⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Marked microcytosis with relatively preserved/high RBC count

Why it happens

Inherited reduced alpha or beta globin synthesis.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Target cells; beta trait often raised HbA2; distinguish from iron deficiency.

Aplastic Anaemia⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Pancytopenia; recurrent infection; bleeding; fatigue

Why it happens

Bone-marrow stem-cell failure yields hypocellular fatty marrow.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Reticulocytes low; no splenomegaly in typical disease; urgent specialist evaluation.

Haemophilia⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Deep muscle bleeds; recurrent haemarthroses; prolonged aPTT

Why it happens

X-linked factor VIII (A) or IX (B) deficiency.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Platelets and PT usually normal; avoid traumatic procedures without haemostatic planning.

von Willebrand Disease (vWD)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Epistaxis; menorrhagia; gum bleeding; prolonged bleeding after procedures

Why it happens

Defective platelet adhesion from low/dysfunctional vWF; VIII may also fall.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Mucocutaneous bleeding vs haemophilia deep-tissue bleeding; platelet count usually normal.

Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC)⌄
Classic stem / buzzwords

Sepsis/obstetric emergency + oozing + microthrombi

Why it happens

Uncontrolled coagulation consumes platelets and clotting factors.

EXAM PEARL & SAFETY TRAP

Low platelets/fibrinogen, raised D-dimer, prolonged PT/aPTT; treat underlying trigger urgently.
